Our farm

A small farm with a funny name and a serious standard.

Veteran owned · Family operated

Fluffy Butt Farm is Allen Hager's pastured-poultry farm on Wolf Run Road in Cameron, out in the Marshall County hills. Every bird is raised outdoors on grass — meat chickens in moveable tractors, laying hens free-ranging the pasture and woods, and turkeys out on the field.

It's hands-on, year-round work. When the 2024 drought dried up the hills, Allen told The Intelligencer he hadn't mowed in a month and a half because every blade of grass went to the chickens:

“The chickens get moved a lot more often now because they just eat up the grass as fast as I put them on it.”

Allen Hager, owner — The Intelligencer, Wheeling, Sept. 2024

The flock keeps growing: Blue Slate turkeys joined the Broad Breasted Whites this year, and Black Copper Marans crosses are coming up through the laying flock. Thank you to every customer who made last year our biggest yet.

How we raise them

Grass, sunshine, and room to roam.

You can taste the difference when a bird spends its life outside. Here's what that looks like on our farm.

01

Moved to fresh pasture daily

Our meat chickens live in open-sided chicken tractors that get rolled onto new grass every day — clean ground, fresh greens and bugs, and shade overhead.

02

Truly free-range hens

Our layers roam the pasture and can duck into the woods for cool shade on hot days — happy hens that kept laying right through the 2024 drought.

03

Pasture-raised turkeys

Broad Breasted Whites, and now Blue Slates too, raised out on grass for the holiday table and for our ground turkey.
